# Contributing to ljer

Contributions to this project must be accompanied by a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). You (or your employer) must sign a CLA to declare that you have the right to grant us a license to use your contributions.

* If you are an individual writing original source code and you own the intellectual property, you must sign an [individual CLA](https://cla.developers.google.com/).
* If you work for a company that allows you to contribute your work, your company must sign a [corporate CLA](https://cla.developers.google.com/).

Please follow the instructions on these pages to sign and submit the agreement electronically. Once we receive your signed agreement, we can accept your pull requests.

## Community Guidelines

All submissions to Google Open Source projects must follow Google’s Contributor License Agreement (CLA). This doesn’t prohibit the use of coding assistance tools, including tool-, AI-, or machine-generated code, as long as these submissions abide by the CLA's requirements.
